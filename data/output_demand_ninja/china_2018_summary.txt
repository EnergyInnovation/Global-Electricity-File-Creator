China 2018 - Demand.ninja hourly heating / cooling electricity
================================================================
Model         : Staffell, Pfenninger and Johnson (2023), Nature Energy
Code          : github.com/renewables-ninja/demand-ninja (local clone)
Weather       : renewables.ninja MERRA-2 reanalysis, province level
Aggregation   : per-province demand, population-weighted via
                province population in APAC W/(C*person) coefficients
Total pop.    : 1396.1 million (sum of 31 province values)

Parameters (APAC averages, Supplementary Table 4)
  T_heat      : 11.9 degC
  T_cool      : 20.4 degC
  P_heat      : 29.0 W/(degC*person)
  P_cool      : 52.0 W/(degC*person)
  smoothing   : 0.73
  solar gains : 0.014 degC/(W/m^2)
  wind chill  : -0.12 degC/(m/s)
  humidity    : 0.036 degC/(g/kg)

Annual totals
  Heating     : 1,053.7 TWh (1,053,689,832,601 kWh)
  Cooling     : 501.8 TWh (501,758,637,818 kWh)
  Combined    : 1,555.4 TWh

Peak hourly demand
  Heating     : 628.3 GW
  Cooling     : 384.2 GW

Caveats (read before using)
  - China was not in the paper's calibration set; APAC-region
    averages (n=5) are used as a proxy. Heating power is high
    because the APAC sample is heating-electrified (Japan, etc.);
    real Chinese electric heating is much lower in the north
    where district heating runs on coal/gas.
  - Output represents WEATHER-DRIVEN demand for a generic
    APAC-style building stock. Calibrate against IEA or China
    Electricity Council data before using as an estimate of
    actual Chinese demand.
  - Province population mapping was inferred from temperature
    patterns; verify against renewables.ninja source docs.
  - The paper does not split commercial vs residential.
  - All numbers should be verified against primary sources.
