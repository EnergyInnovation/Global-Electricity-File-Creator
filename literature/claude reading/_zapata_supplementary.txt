6.2 End-use stylized functions and calibration of the model 
In this section the end-use stylized functions and the hourly patterns obtained are described and compared against the empirical end-use data. Furthermore, monthly variation of the modelled patterns obtained are illustrated in this section for the year 2019. The stylized functions are formulated for the end-uses demand patterns with climate dependence from the residential and service sector. The resulting day patterns from each of the stylized functions, varying per month and type of day, are normalized to the daily sum of electricity demand. Thereafter, an electricity demand ratio between weekday and weekend obtained from the empirical data is applied. Finally, a pattern describing the monthly variation is implemented which is obtained from the climate parameter used for each end-use.

Space Heating 
Electric heating hourly pattern is known to depend on the outside temperature [49]. However, there was no relation found between the reference USA database available for this end-use [29] and the parameters implemented for making the stylized functions (hourly biophysical parameter (HDH) and the service occupancy profile). Therefore, the monthly patterns from the reference USA database were implemented for all regions for weekday and weekend days.
